Figure 4. A, Pooled empirical one-second termination probability versus run age, with physical-fixture bootstrap uncertainty. The age-only baseline and full hazard model are shown through the supported 0-35 s range, with lighter dashed continuation outside support. Open grey symbols denote sparse bins. B, Pooled empirical termination probability stratified by recent Low, Mid and High collective-order state using the trailing five-second order state. C, Leave-one-physical-fixture-out observed-path survival reconstruction. At each fold, hazard parameters were estimated from all other physical fixtures and applied to the excluded fixture; order-dependent models used the observed held-out order sequence as a declared time-varying covariate. Points show held-out empirical survival with physical-fixture bootstrap uncertainty.
